# Blockchain Use in Harmony Genius

Harmony Genius uses blockchain in a hybrid and practical way.

## Why blockchain is needed

Music creators in emerging ecosystems often struggle to prove authorship and maintain transparent attribution when their works are shared digitally.

Traditional centralized databases can store ownership information, but they depend on trust in a single platform and may be altered or disputed.

## Blockchain role

Harmony Genius uses blockchain only in the background for:

- Creator ownership registration
- Timestamped song metadata records
- Transparent royalty attribution logs

## What does NOT run on blockchain

To maintain simplicity and accessibility:
- Streaming does not run on blockchain
- Mobile-money payments do not run on blockchain
- User interaction does not run on blockchain

## Value of this model

This hybrid architecture allows Harmony Genius to:
- protect creators
- improve trust
- maintain transparency
- remain usable in low-resource mobile environments
