# harmony-genius-platform
Harmony Genius is a mobile-first music collaboration platform enabling creators and choirs to create, collaborate, monetize, and securely register music ownership using a hybrid blockchain infrastructure.
Harmony Genius is a mobile-first digital platform designed to support collaborative music creation, choir rehearsal coordination, creator monetization, and transparent music ownership records across emerging music ecosystems.

The platform is being developed to serve choir singers, choir directors, composers, solo artists, and music listeners through a structured ecosystem that combines:

- Music Reels for short musical video content
- Choir Workspace for rehearsal coordination and collaboration
- Creator Upload Studio for publishing music
- Music Marketplace for discovery and monetization
- Blockchain-based transparency for creator ownership and royalty attribution

## Problem

Across many African and emerging music communities, creators and choirs rely on fragmented tools such as messaging apps to share rehearsal materials and publish music. These tools do not support structured collaboration, creator ownership, or transparent attribution.

## Solution

Harmony Genius introduces a dedicated music collaboration platform where musicians can create, collaborate, earn, and own their music.

## Hybrid Blockchain Model

Harmony Genius uses a hybrid architecture:
- Streaming, user interaction, and payments run on conventional cloud infrastructure
- Blockchain is used in the background for creator ownership registration and transparent royalty attribution logs

## Current Status

The project is currently in MVP planning and architecture development stage. Product UI concepts, technical scope, and implementation roadmap have been prepared.
