# Harmony Genius Product Roadmap

## Phase 1: MVP Foundation
Target: Initial pilot-ready mobile platform

Core features:
- User onboarding and profiles
- Audio feed and playback
- Creator upload studio
- Music Reels
- Choir Workspace Lite
- Music Marketplace
- Basic creator ownership registry
- Blockchain-based attribution model design

## Phase 2: Platform Expansion
Target: Stronger collaboration and creator economy

Planned features:
- Expanded choir workspaces
- Creator support and monetization tools
- Hymnal integration
- Enhanced social interaction
- Creator dashboards
- Blockchain ownership registration live integration

## Phase 3: Intelligent Music Infrastructure
Target: Deep music technology differentiation

Planned features:
- AI harmony generation
- Voice note recognition
- SATB assistance
- Virtual rehearsal support
- Advanced royalty and attribution automation
